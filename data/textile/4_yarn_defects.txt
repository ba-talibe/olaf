Yarn Defects
Yarns are a significant source of defects and must be carefully monitored. Common defects include:

Localized yarn irregularities: Yarns are not even

Twisting defect: Inappropriate twist for the yarn

Thin yarn: Section of yarn with lower linear mass than standard

Thick yarn: Section with higher linear mass than standard

Slub or thick spot: Noticeable thickened section

Snarl: Small twisted loop formed by a yarn

Traveling yarn: Yarn winding around smaller elements

Foreign yarns: Yarns differing from the main ones

Soiled yarns: Abnormally colored yarns
