Dyeing, Printing, or Finishing Defects or Characteristics
During dyeing or printing, several defects may appear. The main ones are:

Finishing bar

Finishing stripe

Crease: False fold

Dye stain: Locally altered shade

Printing defect: Irregular print: doctor blade mark, color missing, etc.

Seam mark: Visible seam when it shouldn't be

Pinhole: Tear caused by poor fabric grip
