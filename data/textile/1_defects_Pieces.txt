Defects or Characteristics of Fabric Pieces
First, a piece of fabric can informally be called a "fabric segment." It is in fact a sample from a larger whole. The main defects we find are:

Shade variation: The shade is "non-uniform"

Non-compliant shade: The shade is not the expected one

Pattern irregularity: The pattern is not regularly repeated

Shrinkage defect: Excessive or insufficient shrinkage

Asymmetric pattern distribution: The pattern is not identical on both halves

Defective marking: Not compliant with required standards

Longitudinal waviness: Piece is not straight
