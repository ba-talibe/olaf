Weft Defects
The weft consists of yarns that cross the warp in a back-and-forth motion according to the weave desired. In the referenced image (from “Larousse Encyclopedia 2002”), the weft yarns are green, while warp yarns are blue and red. A pick refers to the weaving unit between weft and warp yarns. Main weft defects include:

Double pick: Two picks are present

Loop: Small folded part of a pick

Broken pick: Cut or snapped weft yarn

Loose pick: Pick longer than neighboring ones

Missing pick: A pick is absent

Skipped pick: Incorrectly inserted pick

Tight pick: Pick shorter than the others

Drawn-in pick: No take-up, may cause puckering

Bar: Narrow stripe differing from the fabric

Weft irregularity: Light bar from under-wefting, tight bar from over-wefting
